Founder · August 2024 - Present

Rewoven

  • 1,000+ garments donated
  • 2.3M litres of water saved
  • 635 brands rated
  • USD 8,000+ to charity

Rewoven is a sustainability initiative I founded to tackle textile waste and raise awareness of circular fashion among young people. It started in a garment factory in Egypt, where a sustainability audit showed me how much fabric ends up as waste. As part of Rewoven I led the product design, branding, and marketing for a student-run project that turned repurposed garment waste into bookmarks and bags and donated more than USD 8,000 in proceeds to charity.

The Rewoven mobile app and website take that further, with interactive sustainability quizzes, games, educational articles, a sustainable fashion explorer that grades 635 brands, a clothing impact scanner, an upcycle marketplace, and donation tracking tools. Rewoven has reached an audience of 500+ people. We partnered with Fashion Revolution UAE and presented Rewoven during Fashion Revolution Week in April 2026. Through our donation drives we have diverted more than 1,000 items of clothing from landfill, donating them through Beit Al Khair Society and saving an estimated 2.3 million litres of water.

Founder · October 2024 - Present

Trade Buddy

  • Website and mobile app
  • Public developer SDK
  • School-wide rollout in progress

Trade Buddy helps families exchange and reuse school items instead of buying new ones. I developed a marketplace where parents can buy, sell, and donate textbooks, uniforms, and equipment within their school community, cutting household waste and unnecessary purchases. I'm now preparing a school-wide rollout through the school's communication channels, so that more families take part and more items get reused.

Sustainability Analyst · July 2024, 2025 & 2026

Textile Sustainability Audits, Embee International Industries

  • 3 summers on site
  • Egypt

Over three summers I have carried out sustainability audits of textile production at Embee International Industries, a textile and garment manufacturer in Egypt. I assessed fabric sourcing, energy and water consumption, waste generation, and management practices, and analysed textile waste streams by source and material to find opportunities for reuse, recycling, and material recovery.

I developed resource-efficiency and circularity recommendations to reduce textile waste and presented the findings to management. Seeing industrial textile waste first-hand, and how much of it could be reused or redistributed, is what led me to start Rewoven.
