Tether

  • 1st of 221 teams
  • Venture Cup 2026 regional finalist
  • Patients, clinicians & caregivers

Tether began in spring 2026 as my team's entry to the New York Academy of Sciences' Junior Academy Challenge on Human Centered AI. We placed 1st of 221 teams. It is a clinical companion app for patients who have just been discharged from hospital: clinicians publish a plain-language recovery plan, patients complete a daily check-in in under 30 seconds, and red flags are escalated to the care team.

After the win we set up Avlynor to take Tether forward, and I am its Co-Founder and CTO. I led product development end to end: a cross-platform app for patients, clinicians, and caregivers; a Cloudflare Workers backend with secure authentication and role-based access; and a voice-analysis engine. The voice work is an active research programme, not a validated clinical tool. We are investigating whether changes in a patient's voice, measured against their own baseline, can give an earlier signal of deterioration. We are now working towards a first pilot in hospital discharge wards in Sweden, and Avlynor was a Venture Cup 2026 regional finalist, one of 10 startups selected in Region Syd from 264 entries nationwide.

Dubai Science Fair 2026 · 1st Runner-Up & Best Innovation Award

J.A.R.V.I.S., a macOS Voice Assistant

  • 45+ modules
  • TypeScript, Rust & Swift
  • Open source

J.A.R.V.I.S. is an open-source AI assistant for macOS that I built and entered in the Dubai Science Fair 2026, where it was named 1st Runner-Up and won the Best Innovation Award among 70 projects. It combines wake-word voice control, a Spotlight-style launcher, screen awareness, and browser automation, and it connects to companion apps on Apple Watch and iPhone.

Under the hood it is a TypeScript core with a Rust sidecar and native Swift apps. Commands pass through a seven-phase parser, so exact matches resolve locally and the language model is only a last resort. More than 45 modules cover tasks from app and window control to calendar, email, and research.

Dubai Science Fair 2025 · Best Prototype Award

Automated Sustainable Desk Assistant

  • Raspberry Pi 5
  • Python
  • Best Prototype Award

My 2025 Dubai Science Fair project was a desk assistant built on a Raspberry Pi 5 and programmed in Python. It monitors room temperature with a DHT11 sensor, shows task and break reminders on an LCD, and switches a desk lamp on or off depending on ambient light, so the light is only on when it is needed.

It won the Best Prototype Award among 55 students.

Payroll Automation, Hello Chef

During an internship at Hello Chef, a meal-kit company in Dubai, I documented the end-to-end monthly payroll process for 60+ employees and built a prototype tool that generates and validates the UAE Wage Protection System (WPS) bank file directly from the payroll sheet.

I also wrote a four-stage automation proposal covering payroll, HR-system integration, and expense approvals, based on a structure worked out with the finance team. Separately, I suggested ways to reduce food waste in operations.
