Sport & Community

Competitive basketball, sailing, high-altitude expeditions, and the community work that has grown out of them.

Organiser · December 2025 & August 2026

Basketball Community Initiative, Manila

  • 30 young players coached
  • 50+ people supported

I organised and led basketball training sessions for 30 underprivileged young people in Manila, Philippines, using the sport to promote physical well-being, teamwork, and confidence. Alongside the sessions I piloted a clothing redistribution scheme, donating sports jerseys to young people through a community basketball programme that supports 50+ people.

2022 - Present

Basketball

  • PBA U18 Youth League champions
  • MVP, BBA U16
  • DASSA U16 runners-up

I have represented Dubai College in the U14, U16, and U19 teams over 4+ years of competitive training and league play. Along the way I won the Philippine Basketball Association U18 Youth League, was named Most Valuable Player in the BBA U16 Youth League in 2024, and finished 2nd in the Dubai Affiliated School Sports Association (DASSA) U16 League.

2022 - Present

Sailing

I am a certified sailor at Dubai Offshore Sailing Club, where I train for three hours a week and was selected for the youth racing squad.

March - April 2024

Everest Base Camp & Expeditions

  • AED 50,000 raised
  • 4 high-altitude expeditions

In spring 2024 I completed the Everest Base Camp expedition, raising AED 50,000 to support the refurbishment of a special-needs school, Jad’s Inclusion, whose banner we carried to base camp. My other high-altitude expeditions include Mount Meru, Mount Washington, and Mount Rainier.

2024

Duke of Edinburgh Bronze Award

I completed the Duke of Edinburgh Bronze Award in 2024.
