Sport & Community

Outside the classroom, I play competitive basketball, sail, and trek, and some of my community work has grown out of these activities.

Organizer · December 2025 & August 2026

Basketball Community Initiative, Manila

  • 30 young players
  • 50+ jerseys donated

In December 2025 and August 2026, I organized and led basketball training sessions for 30 underprivileged young people in Manila, Philippines. We used the sport to promote physical well-being, teamwork, and confidence. Alongside the sessions, I led a pilot clothing drive through Rewoven, which donated more than 50 jerseys made from fabric offcuts to children in Manila.

2022 - Present

Basketball

  • DASSA U16 runners-up
  • PBA U18 Youth League winning team
  • MVP, Beyond Basketball Academy U16

Since 2022, I have represented Dubai College in its U14, U16, and U19 teams through more than four years of competitive training and league play. Along the way, my school team finished second in the Dubai Affiliated School Sports Association U16 League. In the Philippines, I also played for the team that won the Philippine Basketball Association U18 Youth League. In 2024, I was also named Most Valuable Player in the Beyond Basketball Academy U16 Youth League.

2022 - Present

Sailing

Since 2022, I have trained for three hours a week at Dubai Offshore Sailing Club, where I am a certified sailor and was selected for the youth racing squad.

March - April 2024

Everest Base Camp Trek

  • AED 50,000 raised

In spring 2024, I completed the Everest Base Camp trek with my brother and raised AED 50,000 towards the refurbishment of Jad's Inclusion, a special-needs school. We also carried the school's banner with us to base camp. I have also reached the summits of Mount Meru, Mount Washington, and Mount Rainier.

2024

Duke of Edinburgh Bronze Award

For my Duke of Edinburgh Bronze Award in 2024, I tutored younger students in computer science. My expedition project compared the fauna and flora of the desert with those of the city of Dubai.
