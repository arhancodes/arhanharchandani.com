About Me

Hi! I'm Arhan, a Year 13 student at Dubai College. My main interest is where sustainability, technology, and data meet: building software that makes sustainable choices easier, and researching what actually changes people's behaviour. That is the idea behind Rewoven, the circular-fashion initiative I founded. I also built Tether with my team, a clinical companion app that won the New York Academy of Sciences' Junior Academy Challenge out of 221 teams.

I'm currently pursuing my A-Levels in Economics, Physics, and Mathematics, alongside an Extended Project Qualification. I also write a lot of code (JavaScript, TypeScript, Swift, Rust, Go, and Elixir), carry out sustainability audits at a textile manufacturer in Egypt, and study how people act on their concern about the climate. My market study on sustainable clothing adoption was published in the Curieux Academic Review.

Beyond the classroom, I play competitive basketball, sail with the youth racing squad at Dubai Offshore Sailing Club, and trek at high altitude, including the Everest Base Camp trek, for which I raised AED 50,000 towards the refurbishment of a special-needs school. I also founded Dubai College's Big Data and Social Media Society and was selected to speak at TEDxDubai College.
