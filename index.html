About Me

In the summer of 2024, a sustainability audit that I carried out at a garment factory in Egypt showed me how much fabric ends up as waste. This is why I founded Rewoven, an initiative that teaches young people about textile waste and has collected more than 1,000 garments for donation.

However, my survey of 55 people in the UAE later showed me that knowing about a problem does not always change what people do. Among respondents who rated their sustainable-fashion knowledge highly, 17.2 percent still disposed of their clothes unsustainably. This gap between knowledge and action is also what draws me to behavioral economics in my A-Level Economics course.

I am also interested in how data and algorithms shape our choices. After a two-week course in Big Data & Social Media at Johns Hopkins University, I founded the Big Data and Social Media Society at Dubai College. Additionally, my team and I built Tether, a clinical companion app for patients who have just left hospital. It placed first among 221 teams in the New York Academy of Sciences' Junior Academy Challenge.

Beyond the classroom, I play competitive basketball and sail with the youth racing squad at Dubai Offshore Sailing Club. In spring 2024, I also completed the Everest Base Camp trek with my brother and raised AED 50,000 towards the refurbishment of Jad's Inclusion, a special-needs school.

Looking ahead, I hope to study environmental science and data at university. Ultimately, I would like to research the gap between what people know and what they do, and to build tools that make sustainable choices easier.
