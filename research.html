From Concern to Choice: Climate Change and Sustainable Clothing Adoption

  • 55 respondents
  • Published Oct 2026, p. 443
  • Gold CREST Award

I wanted to understand why people who care about climate change still buy and dispose of clothes the same way, so I designed and ran a cross-sectional survey as a market study for Rewoven. The 55 responses, most of them from the UAE, covered consumer demand, sustainability awareness, donation behaviour, and the barriers to adopting a circular-fashion platform.

Two results stood out. Among respondents who rated their sustainable-fashion knowledge highly, 17.2% still reported disposing of clothes unsustainably: a knowledge-behaviour gap. And 66.7% of respondents who were not donating their clothes said they would use a donation feature, which supports convenience-focused tools such as donation locators and clothing-label scanners. The sample is small, so I treat the findings as exploratory.

The paper was published in the Curieux Academic Review (October 2026, Issue 12, page 443), and the project earned a Gold CREST Award from the British Science Association.

Founder · January - March 2026

Big Data and Social Media Society, Dubai College

I founded this student-led society at Dubai College to explore how big data, algorithms, and social media shape the way people think and behave. Over eight interactive sessions with 11 students, we looked at consumer behaviour, digital influence, and how data-driven insights can be used to encourage more sustainable choices.
