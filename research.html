Research Author · April - September 2026

From Concern to Choice: Climate Change and Sustainable Clothing Adoption

  • 55 respondents
  • Published Oct 2026, p. 443
  • CREST Gold Award

Between April and September 2026, I designed and ran a cross-sectional survey of 55 people in the UAE as a market study for Rewoven. The survey covered consumer demand, sustainability awareness, donation behavior, and the barriers to adopting a circular-fashion platform. I then tested five hypotheses using Pearson correlation and chi-square tests.

Two results from the survey stood out to me. Among respondents who rated their sustainable-fashion knowledge highly, 17.2 percent still reported disposing of clothes unsustainably, which suggests a gap between knowledge and behavior. Additionally, six of the nine respondents who did not donate their clothes, or 66.7 percent, said they would be willing to donate once practical barriers were removed. This supports building convenient tools, such as donation locators and clothing-label scanners. The sample is small, so I treat the findings as exploratory.

My paper was published in the October 2026 issue of the Curieux Academic Review, Issue 12, starting on page 443. The project also earned a CREST Gold Award from the British Science Association.

In January 2026, after my course at Johns Hopkins, I founded this student-led society at Dubai College. We explored how big data, algorithms, and social media shape the way people think and behave.
